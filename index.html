Your safety

How we keep patients and staff safe

To protect patients, visitors and our team, we have put the following measures in place.

  • Screening patients and visitors for fever, recent travel history, and signs or symptoms of respiratory infection before appointments
  • Having patients pre-rinse with Peridex mouthrinse
  • A strict hand-washing regime
  • Removing magazines from waiting areas
  • An Airborne Infection Isolation Room to extract aerosols
  • A Beyes Duramax Extraoral Suction system to clean and sterilise the air
  • Giving staff appropriate PPE (N95 masks, face shields and disposable gowns as required)

What we ask of you

Please tell us if you have cold or flu-like symptoms, an existing lung condition or immunodeficiency, or if you have travelled overseas in the last month.

Hand washing and hand sanitiser are available at our practice. Please use them.
